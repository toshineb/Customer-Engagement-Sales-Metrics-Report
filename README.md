## Customer Engagement & Sales Metrics Excel Dashboard Dataset

The main purpose of this blog is to give you the dataset to practice building the Customer Engagement & Sales Metrics Excel Dashboard. Use this practice Spreadsheet Template to follow the steps below, then customize the visuals and KPIs for your business needs.
How this fits your Dashboard Templates workflow

The same structure works across professional Dashboard Templates: adapt it as a Google sheets dashboard or a Power BI Dashboard, and present highlights in a PowerPoint Dashboard using your existing Power Point templates.
Customer Engagement & Sales Metrics

Enhance your business insights with the Customer Engagement & Sales Metrics template from Other Levels. This Excel Dashboard empowers teams to analyze customer performance, track sales trends, and optimize decision-making with interactive, data-driven reports.
Key Features

Total Sales Overview for a clear picture of overall sales performance.
Top 20 Customers Analysis

    Contribution Percentage to total sales
    Total Sales, Total Offers, Total Orders breakdown
    Success Rate to evaluate engagement quality
    Column Chart visualizing orders and sales for the top 20 customers

Dynamic Customers List Table

    Customer Names with detailed sales insights
    Total Sales & Contribution Percentage to revenue
    Sales Growth trends over the last three years
    Offer-to-Order Conversion Rate for performance evaluation

Interactive Slicers & Navigation

Two Slicer Filters to filter by Year and Customer Name, plus Navigation Buttons to move between the data table and the report.
Customization & Editing

All data and values are fully customizable, making this template flexible for tracking customer engagement and sales performance across different industries.
Package Includes

    Three sheet tabs: Dataset, Formula sheet, Dashboard
